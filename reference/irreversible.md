# Irreversible actions

These ask for confirmation and cannot be undone. The product says the irreversible part first.

- **Delete…** a memory: everything it learned goes with it, then what depends on it.
- **Forget** a conversation: the transcript is deleted here and in the assistant.
- Delete a source's imported material.
- **Delete account**: two steps, type the identifier.

Everything else, including **Disconnect…**, **Revoke**, **Remove**, **End** and **Pause**,
can be redone or reversed later.
