# Keep a memory up to date on a schedule

1. On the memory's page, press **Settings**.
2. Under *Schedule*, press **Add schedule**.
3. Pick hourly, daily, weekly, monthly or custom cron. Times are UTC; the local time is shown
   beside it.

The schedule appears on the **Schedules** page, where you can pause or retune it. Each run's
result lands on Activity, and in your Telegram chat if one is bound.
