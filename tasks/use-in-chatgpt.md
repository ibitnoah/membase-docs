# Use a memory in ChatGPT

1. **Connect › ChatGPT.** The steps for ChatGPT unfold.
2. In ChatGPT, open **Settings › Plugins**, press **+** and paste the connector URL.
3. ChatGPT opens the browser sign-in. Approve access in Membase and tick the memories.
4. Back in ChatGPT, switch the Membase connector on in a chat and ask.

The tile now says *Connected*. Its page lists the memories under **Uses**.
